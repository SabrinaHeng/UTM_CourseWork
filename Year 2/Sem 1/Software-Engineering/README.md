# 📝 Assignment 1 – Reflection

Assignment 1 involved academic writing on the **Rational Unified Process (RUP)**, an iterative software development methodology that emphasizes architecture-centric development, risk management, and continuous improvement. Through researching and writing this paper, I gained a deeper understanding of software engineering methodologies and their importance in managing complex software projects.

Comparing RUP with Agile methodologies also allowed me to appreciate that different projects require different approaches depending on their requirements and complexity. Overall, this assignment improved my academic writing skills, research abilities, and understanding of software development methodologies, which have become valuable foundations for subsequent software engineering and system development courses.

# 🚀 Course Project – Reflection

As an extension of the System Analysis and Design (SAD) course, my team worked on enhancing the **Document Management System (DMS) for Koperasi Kakitangan KADA**. This project was integrated with knowledge gained from **Database** and **System Development** courses, allowing me to experience the complete process of software analysis and design.

Throughout the project, I learned how to analyze user requirements and translate them into various system models and diagrams. I gained practical experience in creating **Use Case Diagrams**, **Use Case Descriptions**, **Activity Diagrams**, **Sequence Diagrams**, and **Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERD)** to represent system functionalities and workflows. These diagrams helped me understand how different components interact and how systems are structured before implementation.

The project also introduced me to Agile development practices, where tasks were divided into smaller iterations and collaboration among team members played an important role in ensuring project progress. Through this experience, I realized the importance of communication, requirement analysis, and proper documentation in software development.

In addition, integrating concepts from the Database course enabled me to design relationships between entities and understand the importance of database normalization and data management. Knowledge gained from the System Development course further strengthened my understanding of project planning, software development methodologies, and system implementation processes.

Overall, this project provided valuable hands-on experience in applying theoretical concepts to solve real-world problems. It enhanced my analytical thinking, teamwork, and problem-solving skills while giving me a deeper appreciation for the importance of system analysis, software engineering, and database design in developing effective information systems.

# 📝 Reflection

Year 2 marked a significant transition from learning fundamental computing concepts to applying them in more practical and complex scenarios. Through courses such as Object-Oriented Programming, Data Structures & Algorithms, Database, and Software Engineering, I gained a deeper understanding of how modern software systems are designed, developed, and maintained.

One of the most valuable lessons from this year was learning to think beyond writing code. I began focusing on software architecture, system requirements, database design, and development methodologies. Working on larger projects taught me the importance of planning, documentation, collaboration, and writing maintainable code.

Database-related courses also strengthened my interest in the Data Engineering field. Understanding how data is stored, managed, and processed gave me insight into the critical role data plays in modern applications and business decision-making.

Beyond technical knowledge, Year 2 helped me improve my teamwork, communication, project management, and problem-solving skills through group assignments and software development projects. These experiences prepared me for more advanced Data Engineering topics and real-world industry challenges in the years ahead.

Overall, Year 2 was a transformative year that bridged the gap between foundational knowledge and professional software development practices, further shaping my career aspirations in software engineering, data engineering, and technology innovation.

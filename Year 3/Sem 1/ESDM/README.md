# 📝 Assignment 1 – Reflection

Assignment 1 focused on analyzing Grab as a digital platform ecosystem. Through this assignment, I learned how different systems within Grab interact and exchange data to provide seamless services to users. I explored the integration points between various stakeholders, including customers, merchants, drivers, and payment gateways, and studied how information flows between these components.

In addition, I gained experience in creating activity diagrams to model business processes and learned to distinguish between logical and physical architecture. Analyzing system failures and proposing improvements also helped me understand the importance of reliability, scalability, and system integration in enterprise systems.

Overall, this assignment enhanced my ability to analyze complex systems and strengthened my understanding of how digital enterprises coordinate multiple components to deliver services efficiently.

# 📝 Assignment 2 – Reflection

Assignment 2 introduced me to Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems through SAP and allowed me to explore how digital enterprises utilize integrated systems to support business operations. By using the SAP Learning Hub and studying PPG's implementation of SAP, I gained a better understanding of how enterprise systems help organizations streamline and automate processes.

One of the most interesting topics was the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process, which illustrated how procurement activities are connected with purchasing, inventory management, and financial processes. Through this assignment, I realized the importance of integration and data consistency across different business functions.

Overall, this assignment broadened my understanding of digital enterprise systems and showed me how technologies such as SAP support operational efficiency and decision-making in large organizations.

# 🚀 Course Project – Reflection

The course project focused on developing a **Subject Registration Enhancement Module** for Universiti Tun Hussein Onn Malaysia (UTHM). The project aimed to address issues in the existing subject registration process, such as manual procedures, isolated data, and the lack of real-time validation, by proposing a centralized and integrated solution based on Enterprise Architecture principles.

Through this project, I gained practical experience in applying software development methodologies, particularly the Waterfall model, to analyze requirements and design system solutions. I also learned how to use Figma to develop high-fidelity prototypes and understand the importance of user-centered design in improving user experience.

In addition, the project provided me with hands-on experience in creating system models such as Use Case Diagrams and Sequence Diagrams to represent system interactions and workflows. I also had the opportunity to explore SAP technologies and understand how enterprise systems support organizational processes.

Working on this project enhanced my analytical thinking, teamwork, and communication skills. More importantly, it helped me understand how enterprise systems are designed to solve real-world problems by integrating business processes and information systems. Overall, this project strengthened my understanding of Enterprise Architecture, software development methodologies, and digital transformation, which are valuable knowledge for my future career in Data Engineering and Information Systems.
